What is being counted?

One chunk fixes a starter rank, pone score, dealer score and lead rank, covering the model’s possible holds at that position. “Built” requires verified local full-depth bytes and, when publication is configured, a matching production acknowledgment. “Published” counts verified acknowledgments for two-reply chunks, not live cache hits. “Archived” counts a separate verified TerraMaster snapshot and may lag behind the build.

Build percentage is the fraction of the selected chunk target completed. Opening coverage is an empirical estimate from held-out games, not a guarantee about future play and not the fraction of all possible positions. Gaps in completion are excluded conservatively.

How to read the confidence bands

The ordinary interval is a paired normal approximation, useful at a predetermined analysis point. The confidence sequence uses fixed mixtures of bounded betting processes, with 2.5% error allocated to each tail. Under the fixed-matchup, constant conditional-mean assumption, its entire sequence covers the true mean with at least 95% probability. It accounts for repeated checking of this matchup, not selection among multiple model experiments.

Scoring, WP calibration and opening-time bands are ordinary 95% pointwise intervals, not confidence sequences. Means are weighted by recorded games, hands or calls. Their uncertainty is clustered by reciprocal deal pair, preserving dependence among decisions from the same games and between the models. They can be unreliable with few pairs and do not justify stopping after repeated checks. Timing intervals describe this run’s workload, not a controlled speed comparison.
